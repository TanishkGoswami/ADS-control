# UI / 06 - Vendor Detail View

## 1. Components
- **Vendor Credit Summary:** Total Credit Limit, Current Outstanding Payable, Available Margin.
- **Assigned Cards & Accounts:** List of payment methods and mapped Meta ad accounts.
- **Pending Settlement Sheet:** Breakdown of un-settled charges by date and ad account.
- **Action Buttons:** `Generate Settlement Invoice`, `Mark Payout Complete`.
